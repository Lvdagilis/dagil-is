Privacy notice

The short version: the contact form is the only thing here that collects anything, and it collects only what you type into it. No analytics, no tracking, no cookies.

Who is responsible

This site is operated by Lukas Vytautas Dagilis, working as an individual activity (individuali veikla) registered in Lithuania and based in Vilnius. For anything about this notice, write to info@dagil.is.

What is collected

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, your email address, and your message. There is no account, no login, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind on this site.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which records standard server request logs — including your IP address — as part of serving any page. That happens for every visitor, whether or not you use the form.

Why, and on what legal basis

Form submissions are used for one purpose: to read your enquiry and reply to it. The basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — steps taken at your request before entering into a contract — or, where your message is not about engaging me, the legitimate interest in Art. 6(1)(f) in answering someone who has written to me.

Sending the form is entirely voluntary. There is no obligation to provide any of it, and the only consequence of not doing so is that I cannot reply.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within 24 months. Enquiries that do lead to one become part of that engagement's record and follow the retention periods set out in my data retention policy instead.

GitHub's server logs are kept for whatever period GitHub applies to them, which is outside my control.

Who else sees it

Two providers, both acting on my instructions:

Form
Formspree, Inc.

Receives and stores what you submit through the contact form, and forwards it to me by email.

Hosting
GitHub, Inc.

Hosts the site and records the server logs described above.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for advertising or profiling. There is no advertising on this site.

Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Both Formspree and GitHub are established in the United States, and Formspree hosts form submissions on servers in the United States — it offers no EU-hosted option. So your data is transferred outside the EU/EEA when you use the form or load a page.

Formspree states that it relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as a data processor, and that it holds SOC 2 Type 2 certification. Its privacy policy is published at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy. Neither provider's terms are an agreement I hold privately — both are public documents, and I will point you at the relevant part of either on request.

If you would rather nothing left the EU, email me at info@dagil.is instead of using the form — an email reaches me directly.

Cookies

None. This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and loads nothing from third-party servers — the only scripts are two local animation files. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, ask that it stop being used for a particular purpose, object to its use, or ask for it in a portable form. Where consent is the basis, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting what happened before.

Write to info@dagil.is for any of these.

If you think your data has been mishandled, you can complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija, vdai.lrv.lt). Raising it with me first is welcome but not required.

Changes

This notice is dated at the top. Material changes update that date.
